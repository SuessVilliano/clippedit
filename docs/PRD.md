# Product Requirements Document

## Product
**Clipped It** — real-time intelligence for livestream culture.

## Problem
Livestream ecosystems move faster than conventional social discovery. Important moments often begin as sudden viewer growth, multiple clips of the same moment, cross-stream topic spread, or a clip with unusually high views per hour. Existing platforms optimize for watching individual creators, not cross-platform intelligence.

## Thesis
Observe lawful/public metadata, normalize it across platforms, store time-series snapshots, calculate velocity/anomaly signals, and detect important moments before total-view leaderboards do. Media production must remain rights-aware and use creator authorization, platform permissions, licensing, embeds, or legitimate editorial workflows.

## Users
- Operators/analysts monitoring AI, markets, business, tech, gaming, or culture.
- Creators who want automatic discovery of their own best moments.
- Clippers/editors working from authorized source media.
- Media brands producing daily/weekly rundowns.
- Brands/sponsors discovering fast-growing creators.

## MVP
- creator watchlists
- live status/current viewers/category/title
- viewer history
- momentum ranking
- public clip metadata where officially supported
- clip velocity
- topic/entity classification
- story clustering
- alerts
- creator authorization state

## Core screens
- `/live`
- `/trending`
- `/emerging`
- `/creators/[id]`
- `/stories/[id]`
- `/alerts`
- `/studio` (later creator-authorized workflow)

## Initial verticals
1. AI / automation
2. Markets / trading
3. Business / SaaS
4. Tech / XR
5. Gaming / culture expansion

## KPIs
- monitored creators
- stream snapshots/day
- detection latency
- breakout detections before leaderboard placement
- alert precision
- connected creators
- generated/approved/published authorized clips

## Non-goals for MVP
- arbitrary third-party video downloading
- automated reposting without rights
- universal scraping where official APIs exist
- full video editor
- bounty marketplace

## Monetization
Free, Pro intelligence, Creator tools, Media/Agency plans, API/data access, and later marketplace transaction fees.

## TradeHybrid.tv
Treat TradeHybrid.tv as a downstream owned distribution destination rather than a core dependency. Clipped It should remain independently useful.
