# Business Model

## Wedge
Start as an operator intelligence tool:

> Tell me who is live, what is accelerating, and what matters before everyone else notices.

This creates the data/history layer before video editing complexity.

## Expansion
### Intelligence subscriptions
Pro/agency/media plans for faster alerts, larger watchlists, deeper history, and exports.

### Creator tools
Authorized moment detection, own-stream clipping, Opus handoff, and publishing.

### Marketplace
Creators post clip bounties; editors compete; Clipped It takes a transaction fee.

### Data/API
Trend intelligence for media companies, sponsors, agencies, and creator managers.

### Owned programming
Use Clipped It intelligence to drive original shows on TradeHybrid.tv and social channels.

## Moat
Potential moat comes from historical time-series, creator baselines, velocity models, cross-platform entity graphs, labeled breakout events, creator/editor marketplace performance, and the distribution feedback loop — not merely calling Twitch/Kick APIs.
